Who We Are

A Legacy of Industrial Excellence

RR Group of Companies stands as a testament to visionary leadership and unwavering commitment to excellence.

Founded in 1995, we have grown from being a modest trading house operating out of Lagos Island to a diversified industrial conglomerate involved in the trading and distribution of various industrial raw material chemicals and plastics polymers; stockists and manufacturers of all types of steel and steel products; manufacturing and conversion of plastic products like PET Preforms, HDPE Caps/Closures, bottles and other plastic products; production of exercise notebooks and all types of packaging products like monocartons, self-adhesive labels, printed blister foil; and stockists of frozen fish and other food products.

Our Vision
